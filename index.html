Who we are

Compassionate care, shaped around the person receiving it.

At Next of Kin Care, we deliver high-quality, personal support to people who need it — whether you're living with a disability, recovering from injury, or navigating the changes that come with ageing. We're here to help you live life to its fullest.

Everything starts with your goals. We build care plans around your preferences so you feel comfortable, secure and genuinely supported — from personal care and household help to community participation and nursing.

Many people from culturally and linguistically diverse backgrounds face extra hurdles when reaching for support. Our workers speak a range of languages and respect the cultural beliefs of everyone in our care.

Our vision

To empower people with disability to live full, independent lives through support that fosters growth, inclusion and opportunity.

Our mission

To deliver client-centred services that promote independence, dignity and wellbeing, helping people connect with their communities and reach their potential.

What we offer

A full range of NDIS supports, under one caring team.

From building everyday skills to managing your plan, we tailor each service to your goals and deliver it with dignity and respect.

Daily Living & Life Skills

Goal-focused support to build confidence and independence in everyday life.

  • Cooking, meal planning & nutrition
  • Budgeting & money management
  • Communication & social skills

NDIS Plan Management

We handle the finances and paperwork so you can focus on your goals.

  • Fast, accurate invoice processing
  • Budget tracking & monthly statements
  • Registered & non-registered providers

Supported Independent Living

Personalised support to live confidently in a safe, comfortable home.

  • Help with daily personal activities
  • Medication reminders & support
  • 24/7 support options where needed

Household Tasks

Reliable, respectful help to keep your home clean, safe and comfortable.

  • Cleaning, laundry & ironing
  • Meal prep & grocery assistance
  • Vacuuming, mopping & tidying

Travel & Transport

Safe, comfortable travel to appointments, work and community life.

  • Medical & therapy appointments
  • Shopping, errands & outings
  • Learning to use public transport

Community Participation

Meaningful, creative ways to connect, learn and build friendships.

  • Social & recreational activities
  • Groups, clubs & local events
  • Volunteering & civic participation

Daily Personal Activities

Respectful help with personal care that protects your dignity.

  • Showering, dressing & grooming
  • Mobility & transfer assistance
  • Meal preparation & eating support

Support Coordination

We connect your plan to the right providers and make it all work together.

  • Understanding & using your plan
  • Coordinating services & appointments
  • Support through plan reviews

Employment & Education

Practical support to reach your work, study and career goals.

  • Resume prep & interview coaching
  • Workplace & on-the-job support
  • Access to training & courses
