Save your images in THIS folder using these exact names (all lowercase):

  hero.jpg              Hero, top of page          Portrait / tall  (2:3)
  about.jpg             About section              Square           (1:1)
  gallery-home.jpg      Gallery - In-home support  Portrait         (2:3)
  gallery-community.jpg Gallery - Community        Portrait         (2:3)
  gallery-transport.jpg Gallery - Travel/transport Portrait         (2:3)
  gallery-skills.jpg    Gallery - Life skills      Portrait         (2:3)

Tips
- Names are case-sensitive on most web servers. Keep them lowercase, exactly as above.
- Images are cropped to fill their frame, so keep the subject roughly centred.
- Compress to ~150-300 KB each (squoosh.app or tinypng.com) so the page loads fast.
- .png or .webp also work, but then update the matching src in index.html.
Until a file exists, the site shows the built-in illustration placeholder instead.
